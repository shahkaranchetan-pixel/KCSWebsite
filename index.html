Why Us

What Makes This Firm Different

A Chartered Accountant firm, not a filing shop

A great deal of what is sold as accounting in Mumbai is data entry with a return attached. The work that changes outcomes — deciding how a transaction should be treated, whether a credit is defensible, what a business is actually worth — requires professional judgement and someone willing to sign their name to it. Every engagement here is led by a Chartered Accountant who carries that accountability.

Credentials you can verify

Our founder, CA Karan Chetan Shah, is an Associate Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI Membership No. 190724) and a Registered Valuer under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Reg. No. IBBI/RV/06/2024/15561, asset class: Securities and Financial Assets), as well as a CFA Level 3 candidate. Both registrations are checkable on the ICAI and IBBI public registers. That combination matters in practice: a valuation for a funding round, a scheme of arrangement or an ESOP grant must come from a Registered Valuer, and most CA firms have to refer that work out.

Cloud-first, and specific about it

We run client accounting on Zoho Books, configured properly for Indian GST — state codes, place-of-supply defaults, HSN and SAC mapping, reverse-charge flags, e-invoicing and e-way bills. Where a business is still on Tally, we migrate it using a documented ledger-mapping and import sequence rather than a bulk CSV dump. See our Zoho Books implementation service and the Tally to Zoho Books migration framework.

The whole compliance calendar, not the famous parts

GST and income tax get attention because they are monthly and visible. What catches Mumbai businesses out is the rest: AOC-4 and MGT-7 at ₹100 per day per form with no cap, DIR-3 KYC, Maharashtra professional tax, MCGM Shops and Establishments registration, and Labour Welfare Fund contributions in June and December. All of it sits on one calendar.
