Who we work with in Fort

Fort and the surrounding Ballard Estate, Horniman Circle and Dalal Street area are home to family-run trading houses, commodity and textile merchants, shipping and customs agencies, stockbrokers and financial intermediaries near the BSE, law firms, and legacy partnerships and HUFs that have operated from the same premises for decades.

Businesses with this much history carry a particular kind of complexity. Books maintained on ageing Tally installations, or on no system at all, need cleaning before they can be audited or migrated. Partnership deeds and HUF structures set up a generation ago often no longer match how the family actually operates, or how the next generation intends to. Property and shareholdings held since well before the indexation rules changed raise real capital gains questions on any transfer or restructuring.

Horniman Circle and the Old Financial District

Our Fort office is at Jeevan Sahakar on Homji Street, just off Horniman Circle. It is a working location rather than a nameplate, and its value is proximity: the Bombay High Court, the Bombay Stock Exchange on Dalal Street, the income tax offices, the Reserve Bank, and a dense concentration of long-established trading houses, brokerages and family businesses are all within walking distance or a short taxi ride.

Assessments, appeals and representation

Faceless assessment moved most first-instance proceedings online, but representation work has not disappeared — it has concentrated. Personal hearings, appellate matters before the Commissioner (Appeals) and the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal, and proceedings that require physical attendance still run through this part of the city. Being ten minutes from the relevant offices makes a practical difference when a hearing is adjourned at short notice or a submission has to be filed the same day.

Legacy trading and family businesses

Fort and Ballard Estate hold a large number of businesses that have traded for decades, often as partnerships or HUFs, sometimes with property held in structures set up two generations ago. The recurring work is succession and restructuring — converting a partnership to an LLP or company without triggering avoidable capital gains, valuing an interest for a family settlement, regularising records that predate any accounting system, and resolving legacy service tax or VAT positions that surface during due diligence.

Capital markets and securities

Proximity to the BSE brings brokerage and investment clients, and with them capital gains computation across large transaction volumes, treatment of speculative and derivative income, and the perennial business-income-versus-capital-gains question on securities trading.
