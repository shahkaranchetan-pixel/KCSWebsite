Compliance That Is Specific to Mumbai

Central taxes are the same everywhere in India. What differs by city is the state and municipal layer, and it is the layer non-local advisers most often miss.

Maharashtra professional tax

Every employer in Maharashtra must register for Profession Tax (both PTEC and PTRC where applicable), deduct professional tax from salaries on the state's slab, and remit it on the prescribed cycle. It is a small monthly amount that accumulates into a meaningful penalty when overlooked for a year, and it is separate from anything the income tax department will remind you about.

Shops and Establishments registration

Businesses operating in Mumbai register under the Maharashtra Shops and Establishments (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act with the MCGM. The registration is commonly required as supporting proof when opening a current account, applying for credit or completing customer onboarding, and renewal deadlines are easy to miss.

Labour Welfare Fund

Maharashtra LWF contributions fall due twice a year, in June and December, with both an employee and employer component. There is no monthly reminder and no return that flags it, which is precisely why it is the item most often found outstanding during due diligence.

GST administration in Mumbai

Maharashtra is state code 27. Mumbai businesses fall under specific Central and State GST commissionerates and divisions depending on the registered address, and it determines which office issues notices and where a personal hearing takes place. Getting the jurisdiction right on registration saves considerable trouble later.

Choosing a CA in Mumbai

Three questions separate firms in practice. First, who actually does the work — a Chartered Accountant, or a junior with a CA signing off at the end? Second, is the relationship monthly or annual? A firm that appears in September to file your return cannot prevent the errors made in March. Third, can they handle the specialist work in-house, or does valuation, FEMA and NCLT work get referred out at your cost?
