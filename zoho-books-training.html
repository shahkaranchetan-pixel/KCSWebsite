What You'll Learn

This free Zoho Books course takes your team from a blank organisation to confident, GST-compliant daily accounting — every lesson is an official Zoho Books video, sequenced by KC Shah & Associates for Indian businesses and CA-firm staff.

Module 1: Getting Started

Sign up, configure your organisation profile, verify your GSTIN and set up the Chart of Accounts — the foundation every Indian business needs before recording a single transaction.

Module 2: Sales, Purchases & Banking

The daily bread-and-butter of accounting: the full quote-to-cash sales cycle and invoicing, the complete accounts payable lifecycle from vendor bills to payments, and bank feed import and reconciliation — three official recent webinars covering the transactions every fresher needs to run before touching migration or GST filing.

Module 3: Migrating Data

Move from Tally or QuickBooks Online into Zoho Books with clean ledger mapping and a trial balance that reconciles to the rupee.

Module 4: Branding & Templates

Customise invoice and estimate templates and add your logo to client emails for professional, consistent communication.

Module 5: GST-Compliant Sales

Build GST-correct invoices with HSN/SAC codes, generate e-way bills, manage TDS liabilities and challans, and record shipping bills for exports.

Module 6: E-Invoicing

Test e-invoicing against the IRP sandbox before going live with B2B e-invoices.

Module 7: GST Return Filing

A four-part masterclass on GSTR-1 preparation, GSTR-2/2B reconciliation, GSTR-3B handling and GSTR-9 annual returns, filed directly from Zoho Books.

Module 8: Reports Masterclass

Eleven official sessions covering business overview reports, receivables and payables, inventory, multi-currency ledgers, budgets and GST reports.

Module 9: Banking & Approvals

Automate payment reminders, set multi-level transaction approvals and manage bank feeds on the go.

Module 10: Automation & Extensions

Building out Zoho Books' custom extension platform, plus the KCS Automation Playbook of bank rules, recurring invoices and approval hierarchies. More extension walkthroughs are being added here regularly.

Module 11: Accounting Fundamentals (Freshers)

One hundred bite-size Accounting 101 lessons for freshers who need the bookkeeping basics before touching the software.
