June 2026 Compliance Calendar

Every statutory due date falling in June 2026 - GST, TDS, income tax, ROC, PF, ESIC and profession tax - with the form, the period it covers and who it applies to.

13 due dates FY 2026-27

June 2026 carries 13 statutory due dates for Indian businesses, spanning GST, payroll, TDS/TCS, income tax and ROC/MCA. The first falls on the 7th (TDS / TCS Deposit) and the last on the 30th (Maharashtra Profession Tax (PTRC)).

The heaviest date is the 15th, when 4 filings fall due together: Advance Tax - Q1 (15%), Form 16 (Salary TDS Certificate), EPF / ECR Payment and ESIC Contribution. Plan the working papers for these a few days ahead - the portals slow noticeably on peak dates.

In total that is 5 GST returns, 1 income tax item and 1 ROC filing, alongside the recurring TDS deposit and payroll obligations. Dates below are indicative and can shift if CBDT, GSTN, MCA, EPFO or ESIC issue an extension circular.
