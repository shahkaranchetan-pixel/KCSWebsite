October 2026 Compliance Calendar

Every statutory due date falling in October 2026 - GST, TDS, income tax, ROC, PF, ESIC and profession tax - with the form, the period it covers and who it applies to.

20 due dates FY 2026-27

October 2026 carries 20 statutory due dates for Indian businesses, spanning GST, TDS/TCS, ROC/MCA, payroll and income tax. The first falls on the 7th (TDS / TCS Deposit) and the last on the 31st (Maharashtra Profession Tax (PTRC)).

The heaviest date is the 15th, when 5 filings fall due together: ADT-1 (Auditor Appointment), Form 16A (TDS Certificate), TCS Return - Form 27EQ, EPF / ECR Payment and ESIC Contribution. Plan the working papers for these a few days ahead - the portals slow noticeably on peak dates.

In total that is 8 GST returns, 1 income tax item and 4 ROC filings, alongside the recurring TDS deposit and payroll obligations. Dates below are indicative and can shift if CBDT, GSTN, MCA, EPFO or ESIC issue an extension circular.
